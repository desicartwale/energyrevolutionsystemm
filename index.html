Meet the Energy Revolution System

The Energy Revolution System is a digital DIY guide, not a physical generator. When you purchase it, you receive access to a main PDF blueprint, diagrams and schematics, a parts and materials list, and video tutorials for the tricky parts—coil winding and magnet alignment.

The project itself is a coil-based electrical circuit inspired by Nikola Tesla’s bifilar coil patent (U.S. Patent No. 512,340, filed in 1894). The sales page frames it as a “forgotten” or suppressed invention, but the honest version is simpler: Tesla’s patent describes a winding method for electromagnets that changes the coil’s electrical properties—it does not describe a generator, and the patent has always been publicly available. What the guide teaches you to build is an experimental circuit that can supplement power for small devices.

Reviewers who have built and tested it report output limited to LED lights and small devices. Constant, low-draw loads like a router, phone chargers, or a small fan are the realistic ceiling.
